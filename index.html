IMPROVED SETUP / v1.0

Ready when you are.

Verify once, manage your account, then choose when to play.

Your account. Your choice to play.

Setup stays open after verification. The game starts only when you tap Play SMC Crows.

Use the account controls to export a private backup or import a saved account. Confirm replacement when switching accounts, and keep a backup of your current account first.

Matching resources are reused on ordinary reopening. Setup checks online for signed resource updates in the background; you choose whether to install an offered update.

Export your account backup outside the app before uninstalling or clearing data. Keep it private.
