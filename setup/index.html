INSTALL AND PLAY

Three steps. Then Play.

No Termux or manual game-folder copying.

  1. Install the APK. Keep 10–15 GB free. For an existing compatible game, back up your account and update over it.
  2. Open Setup. Prepare the resources over Wi-Fi. Follow the original first-start and explicit Crows continuation prompts.
  3. Tap Play SMC Crows. Setup waits for you. It does not automatically launch the game.

Ready when you are.

Check for updates sits below Play. A failed optional check does not block an already verified game.

Touch Riko to replay the synthetic welcome. Rapid taps cannot overlap clips. Use media volume to change loudness or mute.
