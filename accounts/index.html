YOUR ACCOUNT

Keep your progress.

Your valid current account is used automatically. Import only to recover or change it.

Back up first.

  1. In Setup, tap Back up my account.
  2. Save the JSON in Documents or another folder outside app-owned storage.
  3. Keep it private. Treat the file like a password; the export is not encrypted.

Recover or change accounts.

Tap Import account backup, choose your saved JSON and confirm replacement. Existing copies are retained. When ready, tap Play.

What happens after uninstalling?

Uninstalling or clearing app data can erase app-owned account copies. Recovery needs a surviving exported backup and a working game server. A reinstall alone cannot guarantee the old account will return.
